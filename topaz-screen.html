What is a topaz screen?

A topaz screen is a full-display tool that fills your monitor with warm, radiant golden amber (#FFC87C)—a color inspired by the precious topaz gemstone. Topaz is captured afternoon sunlight—warm, golden, and impossibly inviting.

Named after one of the most prized gemstones in history, topaz has been treasured across civilizations for its warm, luminous glow. The color sits gracefully between pure gold and soft peach, carrying the richness of a sunset without the intensity of deep orange. It is the shade of honey held up to the light, of autumn leaves just beginning to turn.

Why Topaz Works for Wellbeing

Research in color psychology shows that warm golden tones like topaz can lower cortisol levels and increase feelings of comfort. Unlike cooler colors that promote analytical focus, topaz encourages a relaxed creativity—the kind of open, free-flowing thinking that leads to breakthroughs.

The Topaz Gemstone Legacy

The topaz gemstone has been revered for thousands of years. Ancient Egyptians believed topaz was colored by the golden glow of the sun god Ra. In medieval Europe, it was thought to heal both physical and mental illness, strengthen the mind, and increase wisdom. The color's warm radiance has made it a symbol of friendship, love, and good fortune across cultures.
