What is a tangerine screen?

A tangerine screen is a full-screen display of the warm, peachy-orange color (#FF9966) that brings citrus energy to any space. Tangerine is sunshine you can taste—warm, sweet, and energizing without the intensity of pure orange.

Tangerine captures the essence of the sweet citrus fruit it's named after—vibrant, warm, and irresistibly inviting. It's orange's friendlier, more sophisticated cousin, with soft pink undertones that make it feel approachable rather than aggressive. While pure orange can feel bold and demanding, tangerine strikes a gentler balance. It energizes without overwhelming, warming any space with the feeling of a perfect sunset or a ripe summer fruit.

Why Tangerine Works for Creative Spaces

Orange tones are proven to stimulate mental activity and creativity. Tangerine's softened intensity makes it sustainable for longer creative sessions—you get the energizing benefits without the visual fatigue that pure orange can cause.

The Citrus Connection

Tangerines were first cultivated in China and later brought to Europe via Morocco's Tangier port (hence the name). The color carries these exotic, sunny origins—evoking warmth, travel, and the simple pleasure of fresh fruit.
