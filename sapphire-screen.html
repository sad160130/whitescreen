What is a sapphire screen?

A sapphire screen is a full-display tool that fills your entire monitor with deep, luminous sapphire blue (#0F52BA)—a rich blue named after the precious gemstone prized for centuries by royalty and seekers of wisdom. Sapphire is the ocean's deepest secret turned into light—a color that carries the weight of the deep sea and the clarity of a flawless gem.

Sapphire blue sits between the brilliance of azure and the gravity of navy, offering a blue that feels both ancient and modern. Throughout history, sapphires have symbolized nobility, truth, and sincerity. The color itself evokes that same sense of enduring value—a blue so deep it seems to hold light within it rather than merely reflecting it.

Why Sapphire Works for Focus

Studies show that blue hues enhance cognitive performance and reduce mental fatigue. Sapphire's particular balance—rich but not overpowering, deep but not dark—creates an environment where the mind can settle into sustained concentration without drifting toward drowsiness or distraction.

The Sapphire Gemstone Legacy

Sapphires have been treasured since 800 BC, when ancient Persians believed the sky was painted blue by the reflection of sapphire stones. Medieval clergy wore sapphires to symbolize heaven, and the gem remains one of the four precious stones alongside diamond, ruby, and emerald. This deep cultural reverence gives sapphire blue an unmatched sense of gravitas and beauty.

What is the hex code for sapphire?

The hex code for sapphire is #0F52BA. Here are the complete technical specifications for our sapphire screen display:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #0F52BA
RGB Values R: 15, G: 82, B: 186
HSL Values H: 216°, S: 85%, L: 39%
CMYK Values C: 92%, M: 56%, Y: 0%, K: 27%
Color Name Sapphire Blue
Color Family Blue

Sapphire (#0F52BA) has very low red (15), moderate green (82), and high blue (186) values. The 216° hue positions it in the deep blue range with subtle azure undertones. At 85% saturation and 39% lightness, sapphire achieves its characteristic gemstone-like richness—vibrant and deep without crossing into darkness.
