Why Lime Feels Electric

Lime's electric quality comes from its pure RGB value (#00FF00 = maximum green, no red or blue). This creates the brightest possible green a digital display can show. Our eyes are most sensitive to green wavelengths, making lime appear exceptionally bright and energetic. This is why lime and similar greens are used in safety gear, traffic signals, and anywhere maximum visibility is needed.
