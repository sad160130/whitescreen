What is a coral pink screen?

A coral pink screen is a full-screen display of warm pink-orange (#F88379) that fills your entire monitor with a soft, inviting glow reminiscent of tropical reefs and golden-hour skies. Coral pink is a sunset blushing over tropical waters—warm enough to embrace, soft enough to soothe.

This delicate blend of pink and orange takes its name from the living coral found in warm ocean waters. Unlike pure pink or orange, coral pink strikes a perfect balance between energy and gentleness, creating a color that feels both vibrant and nurturing at the same time.

What does coral pink do to your mood?

Coral pink occupies a unique emotional space—it combines the nurturing warmth of pink with the energizing optimism of orange. The result is a color that uplifts without overstimulating, comforts without sedating.

Emotional Associations

  • Warmth and comfort — Coral pink evokes feelings of safety, nurturing, and emotional warmth
  • Optimism and joy — The orange undertones bring playful energy and positive feelings
  • Romance and tenderness — Softer than red, coral pink suggests gentle affection and intimacy
  • Creativity and inspiration — Stimulates imaginative thinking without pressure
  • Relaxation and calm — The soft saturation promotes a sense of ease and tranquility

Why Coral Pink Soothes

Color psychology research shows that warm pink tones lower heart rate and reduce feelings of aggression. Coral pink adds a layer of warmth from its orange component, creating a color that feels like a gentle embrace—promoting emotional balance, reducing stress, and fostering a sense of well-being.

Coral in Nature

Coral pink mirrors the colors found in living coral reefs, tropical flowers, and sunset skies. In 2019, Pantone named "Living Coral" its Color of the Year, recognizing coral pink's ability to provide warmth and nourishment in an increasingly digital world.
