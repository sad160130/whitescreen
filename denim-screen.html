What is a denim screen?

A denim screen is a full-screen display of medium-dark blue (#1560BD)—a color that captures the trusted, everyday quality of classic denim fabric. Denim is reliability woven into color—casual, trusted, and never out of style.

Unlike the intense authority of cobalt or the formal gravity of navy, denim blue occupies a comfortable middle ground—approachable yet substantial, relaxed yet purposeful. It's the blue you reach for every day, the one that goes with everything and never tries too hard.

Why Denim Works for Focus

Denim blue sits at a sweet spot in the blue spectrum—bright enough to maintain alertness, deep enough to minimize visual distractions. Its familiar, non-threatening quality reduces cognitive load, allowing your brain to direct energy toward the task at hand rather than processing an unfamiliar environment.

The Denim Heritage

Denim fabric was first produced in Nimes, France (hence "de Nimes") and dyed with indigo. The distinctive medium-blue tone became synonymous with American workwear in the 19th century and evolved into a universal symbol of casual durability. Today, denim blue represents the intersection of utility and style.

What is the hex code for denim?

The hex code for denim blue is #1560BD. Here are the complete technical specifications for our denim screen display:

Color Family Blue

Denim (#1560BD) has low red (21), moderate green (96), and high blue (189) values. The 213° hue places it in the blue range with subtle azure undertones. At 80% saturation and 41% lightness, denim achieves its characteristic medium-dark appearance—brighter than navy, deeper than sky blue, and more muted than pure cobalt.
