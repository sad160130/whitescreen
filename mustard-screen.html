What is a mustard screen?

A mustard screen is a full-display tool that fills your entire device with the color mustard (#FFDB58), creating a warm, vintage-inspired visual environment. Mustard is yellow that grew up and got interesting—it has the optimism of sunshine but with earthy, sophisticated undertones that make it feel like a warm hug from the 1970s.

Mustard (#FFDB58) occupies a unique space in the color spectrum—warmer and more sophisticated than bright yellow, more vibrant than gold, and infinitely more interesting than beige. It's the color of autumn leaves catching afternoon sunlight, of vintage photographs, of cozy sweaters and mid-century modern furniture.

Mustard vs. Similar Colors

Understanding how mustard differs from its neighbors helps you choose the right shade for your needs.

Mustard vs. Yellow: Pure yellow (#FFE600) is brighter and more attention-grabbing. Mustard is muted, more sophisticated, and easier to look at for extended periods. Choose yellow for energy and alerts; choose mustard for warmth and style.

Mustard vs. Gold: Gold (#FFD700) has more metallic, luxurious connotations. Mustard is earthier and more casual. Gold says "celebration"; mustard says "comfortable elegance."

Mustard vs. Amber: Amber (#FFBF00) leans more orange and has traffic-signal associations. Mustard is more yellow-based and evokes nature and vintage aesthetics rather than caution.

Mustard vs. Cream: Cream (#FFFDD0) is much lighter and more neutral. Mustard makes a statement; cream recedes. Use cream for backgrounds and mustard for accents, or use mustard when you want warmth that's actually noticeable.
