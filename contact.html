How Your Feedback Shapes WhiteScreen.wiki

WhiteScreen.wiki isn't built by a large corporation — it's a passion project created to help people find focus in a noisy digital world. Every piece of feedback we receive directly influences how the site evolves.

Here are some features that came from user suggestions:

  • Cream and gray colors — Added after users requested softer alternatives to pure white
  • Keyboard shortcuts — Implemented because users wanted faster access to fullscreen
  • Mobile optimization — Improved based on feedback from tablet and phone users
  • Color psychology content — Expanded after users asked "why do these colors work?"

Your idea could be next. We can't promise to implement everything, but we read every message and genuinely consider how each suggestion could help others.
