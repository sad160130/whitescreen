The Color at the Edge of What Humans Can See

Violet sits at approximately 380-450 nanometers—the highest frequency of light your eyes can detect. One step further and you're in ultraviolet territory. Invisible.

There's something poetic about that. The color artists, mystics, and creatives have gravitated toward for centuries literally exists at the boundary between visible and invisible reality. Make of that what you will.

Violet vs. Purple: Actually Different

Violet is a spectral color—it exists on the rainbow, has a specific wavelength. Purple is a mix of red and blue light that doesn't exist on the spectrum. Violet leans blue. Purple leans red. Your screen can display both, but this page is specifically the spectral one.

Our violet screen gives you pure, fullscreen #8F00FF on any device. The exact shade associated with creativity research, chromotherapy, and the crown chakra in various traditions. Whether you're here for the woo-woo or the wavelength science, the tool works the same.
