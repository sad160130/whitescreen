The Power of Color

Color psychology research shows that different hues can significantly influence our mental state. Here's how each color screen can support your focus and well-being.

White = Clarity

A blank canvas that clears mental noise and promotes fresh thinking. Ideal for writing, brainstorming, and starting new projects.

Blue = Focus

Simple Focus Techniques

Combine color screens with these proven methods to maximize your productivity and calm.

1

The Blue Breath

Before starting a writing session, spend five minutes on the blue screen. Breathe deeply and let your thoughts settle. This primes your mind for focused, creative work.

2

The Green Break

After 45 minutes of work, switch to the green screen for a 3-minute eye rest. Look at the screen, blink slowly, and let the calming color refresh your visual system.

3

The Black Reset

When feeling overwhelmed, enter the black screen fullscreen mode. Close your eyes for one minute, then open them to the darkness. This mental reset can restore clarity.
