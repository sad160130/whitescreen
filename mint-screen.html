What is a mint screen?

A mint screen is a full-display tool that fills your entire device with the color mint green (#98FF98), creating a fresh, clean visual environment for focus, wellness, and relaxation. Mint is green with training wheels—all the calming benefits of nature, but lighter, brighter, and with that unmistakable "fresh start" energy.

Your brain already associates mint with cleanliness: toothpaste, mouthwash, hospital walls, hand sanitizer packaging. Decades of product design have conditioned this response. When you need to feel clear-headed, renewed, or mentally fresh, a mint screen actually delivers those psychological effects.

This isn't manipulation—it's useful. The conditioning works in your favor.

Why Mint Feels Fresh

Mint's freshness comes from multiple associations. The mint plant itself is known for its cooling, refreshing sensation. Mint flavoring in toothpaste and gum reinforces cleanliness associations. The color's position between green (nature) and cyan (water) creates a uniquely crisp, clean impression. These combined associations make mint one of the most "refreshing" colors in our perception.

What is the hex code for mint?

The hex code for mint green is #98FF98. Here are the complete technical specifications for our mint screen display:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #98FF98
RGB Values R: 152, G: 255, B: 152
HSL Values H: 120°, S: 100%, L: 80%
CMYK Values C: 40%, M: 0%, Y: 40%, K: 0%
Color Name Mint Green / Pale Green
Wavelength ~520-560 nm (green range)

Mint (#98FF98) is a light, vivid green with equal red and blue values and maximum green. This creates the fresh, vibrant quality that distinguishes mint from darker, more subdued greens.
