What is an apricot screen?

An apricot screen is a full-display tool that fills your monitor with soft, warm apricot (#FBCEB1)—a gentle pastel orange named after the beloved stone fruit. Apricot is a blanket fresh from the dryer—warm without being hot, soft without being faded.

This nurturing hue lives in the sweet spot between orange and peach, borrowing the cheerfulness of orange and the tenderness of pink while settling into its own quiet comfort. Named after the fruit whose golden-orange flesh has symbolized warmth and abundance for millennia, apricot carries a sense of natural softness and understated elegance that makes any space feel like home.

What does apricot do to your mood?

Apricot occupies a uniquely soothing psychological space—it warms and nurtures without demanding attention or stimulating anxiety. It whispers rather than shouts, creating an emotional environment that feels safe, welcoming, and gently uplifting.

Emotional Associations

  • Comfort and nurturing — Apricot wraps the senses in warmth and gentle care
  • Optimism and joy — Carries the cheerfulness of orange in a softer, quieter form
  • Safety and trust — Creates an approachable, non-threatening atmosphere
  • Creativity and openness — Encourages free thinking without pressure
  • Warmth and hospitality — Associated with welcoming spaces and generosity

Why Apricot Promotes Relaxation

Research in color psychology indicates that warm pastels like apricot lower cortisol levels while maintaining gentle alertness. Unlike cooler tones that can feel isolating, or saturated warm colors that overstimulate, apricot strikes the perfect balance—warm enough to comfort, soft enough to calm. This makes it ideal for evening wind-down routines and mindful moments.

The Fruit Behind the Color

Apricots have been cultivated for over 4,000 years, originating in China before spreading along the Silk Road. The color name "apricot" entered English in the 1850s, describing the distinctive golden-orange blush of the ripe fruit's skin. In many cultures, the apricot symbolizes femininity, gentleness, and timid love.

What is an apricot screen used for?

Relaxation & Ambient Lighting

Apricot creates a warm, nurturing atmosphere when displayed on a screen in your living space. Its soft glow mimics the gentle warmth of golden hour light, making it perfect for evening relaxation, meditation spaces, and creating a cozy environment for reading or unwinding.

Photography & Visual Content

Photographers use apricot as a flattering, warm-toned backdrop for portraits, beauty shots, and product photography. Its soft pastel quality adds warmth without competing with subjects, creating images that feel inviting and naturally lit.

Design & Branding

Apricot works beautifully in brands conveying warmth, approachability, and care. Popular in wellness, skincare, baby products, and hospitality design where comfort and trust are paramount.

Reading & Eye Comfort

A warm pastel like apricot reduces blue light exposure and screen harshness. Use it as a background color for late-night reading or as a warm screen filter to reduce eye strain during evening hours.

Screen & Display Testing

Use an apricot screen to test color accuracy and uniformity on monitors, TVs, and mobile devices. The subtle warm pastel reveals calibration issues in the orange-peach range and helps identify uneven backlighting on light displays.

Other Uses

  • Art reference: Color matching for painting, watercolors, and soft pastel illustration
  • Video production: Warm, flattering backgrounds for beauty and lifestyle content
  • Nursery ambiance: Gentle, soothing light for infant and child spaces
  • Therapy and wellness: Warm pastels are used in chromotherapy for emotional comfort

How is apricot different from other warm pastels?

Apricot occupies a specific niche in the warm pastel family, offering a golden-orange softness distinct from its neighbors. Here's how it compares to help you choose the right shade:

Apricot vs. Peach

Apricot (#FBCEB1): More orange-leaning, warmer and more golden, sun-kissed warmth.

Peach (#FFCBA4): Slightly pinker undertone, more blush-like and rosier. View peach screen.

Apricot vs. Cream

Apricot (#FBCEB1): Richer, more saturated with visible orange warmth.

Cream (#FFFDD0): Lighter, more neutral, barely tinted yellow-white. View cream screen.

Apricot vs. Salmon

Apricot (#FBCEB1): Softer, more pastel, lighter and less saturated.

Salmon (#FA8072): Deeper, more pink-orange, bolder and more vivid. View salmon screen.

Apricot vs. Coral

Apricot (#FBCEB1): Lighter, more muted, gentler pastel quality.

Coral (#FF7F50): More intense, vibrant orange-pink with greater saturation. View coral screen.

Apricot vs. Papaya

Apricot (#FBCEB1): Cooler, more subtle, with delicate golden warmth.

Papaya (#FFEFD5): Lighter, more yellow-leaning, closer to off-white. View papaya screen.
