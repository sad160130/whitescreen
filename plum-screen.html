What is a plum screen?

A plum screen is a full-screen display of deep, rich plum purple (#8E4585) that you can use for luxury design work, sophisticated ambient lighting, photography backgrounds, and creative inspiration. Plum is velvet in color form—soft enough to touch, deep enough to disappear into.

Unlike lighter purples, plum commands attention through richness rather than brightness. It speaks of velvet curtains in grand theaters, vintage wine in crystal glasses, and the quiet confidence that comes with refined taste. It's purple with depth, warmth, and undeniable elegance.

What does plum do to your mood?

Plum combines purple's creative, spiritual qualities with red's warmth and passion. The result is a color that feels both inspiring and grounding, mysterious yet inviting. When you surround yourself with plum, you're tapping into centuries of psychological associations with luxury, wisdom, and emotional depth.

Emotional Associations

  • Luxury and sophistication — Evokes high-end brands and refined taste
  • Creativity and imagination — Stimulates innovative, artistic thinking
  • Mystery and depth — Creates intrigue and emotional richness

Why Plum Works for Sophisticated Spaces

Color research shows that deeper purples are perceived as more luxurious and exclusive. Plum specifically adds warmth to this perception, making spaces feel both elegant and welcoming—a rare combination that high-end brands often seek.

The Royal Connection

Purple dyes were historically among the most expensive to produce, making them exclusive to royalty and the wealthy. Plum carries this heritage, subtly communicating status and refined taste in modern design and branding.

What is the hex code for plum?

The hex code for plum is #8E4585. Here are all the technical color values designers and developers need:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #8E4585
RGB Values R: 142, G: 69, B: 133
HSL Values H: 307°, S: 35%, L: 41%
CMYK Values C: 0%, M: 51%, Y: 6%, K: 44%
Color Name Plum
Color Family Purple / Magenta

Plum (#8E4585) has high red (142), low green (69), and moderate-high blue (133) values. The 307° hue places it in the magenta-purple range, leaning slightly toward red. Moderate saturation (35%) and lower lightness (41%) create its characteristic deep, rich appearance.
