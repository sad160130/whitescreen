Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about WhiteScreen.wiki and how to use color screens for focus and productivity.

WhiteScreen.wiki is a free, ad-free online tool that transforms your browser into a full-screen color environment. Choose from nine calming colors — white, blue, green, black, red, yellow, pink, cream, or gray — to create a distraction-free workspace for focus, meditation, or relaxation.

Think of it as a digital canvas for your mind. No apps to install, no accounts to create. Just open the site, pick a color, and enter fullscreen mode.

Yes, 100% free. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no premium tiers, and no hidden costs. WhiteScreen.wiki was built as a public resource for anyone who needs a calm digital space.

We believe that access to simple focus tools shouldn't be behind a paywall. This site will remain free forever.

For studying and writing, blue and green screens tend to work best. Blue enhances focus and creative thinking, while green reduces eye strain during long sessions. White is also excellent for writing as it provides a clean mental canvas.

That said, everyone's different. We recommend experimenting with different colors to discover what helps you focus best. Learn more about how colors affect focus.

WhiteScreen.wiki automatically remembers your last used color using your browser's local storage. When you return to the site, your preference will be ready to use.

You can also bookmark specific color pages directly. For example, bookmark whitescreen.wiki/colors.html#blue for instant access to your preferred blue screen.

Absolutely. Many users find the black screen ideal for meditation as it eliminates visual stimulation and encourages inward focus. Some prefer softer colors like cream or gray for a gentler experience.

Try entering fullscreen mode, setting a timer on your phone, and focusing on your breath. The absence of visual distractions helps deepen your practice.

Once you've loaded the page, the color screens will continue working even if you lose internet connection. For the best offline experience, you can bookmark the colors page and access your preferred colors anytime.

Since WhiteScreen.wiki is a simple static site with no server-side processing, it works reliably in most conditions.

No. WhiteScreen.wiki does not use any analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. The only data stored is your color preference in your browser's localStorage — and that stays on your device, never sent to any server.

Your privacy matters. We built this site without any tracking because we believe a focus tool should be truly distraction-free, including freedom from surveillance.

We'd love to hear from you! Visit our contact page to send us a message. Whether you have ideas for new colors, focus techniques to share, or just want to say hello — your feedback helps make WhiteScreen.wiki better for everyone.

WhiteScreen.wiki works on any device with a modern web browser — desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. The site is fully responsive and adapts to any screen size.

Fullscreen mode is supported on most desktop and mobile browsers. On some mobile devices, fullscreen behavior may vary slightly depending on the browser.

We see WhiteScreen.wiki as more than just a tool — it's a growing resource dedicated to focus, calm, and the science of color psychology. Like a traditional wiki, our goal is to create a comprehensive, freely accessible knowledge base.
