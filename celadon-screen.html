What is a celadon screen?

A celadon screen is a full-display tool that fills your monitor with soft, refined celadon (#ACE1AF)—a pale grayish-green named after the legendary glazes of ancient Chinese ceramics. Celadon is stillness made visible—quieter than mint, warmer than sage, and infinitely more timeless than either.

This distinguished hue carries centuries of artistic heritage in its gentle green-gray tones. First developed during the Song Dynasty over a thousand years ago, celadon glaze was prized for its jade-like quality—a color so revered that emperors reserved the finest pieces for imperial use. The digital version captures that same sense of refined tranquility and understated sophistication.

Why Celadon Enhances Focus

Research in color psychology shows that soft, desaturated greens like celadon reduce cortisol levels and lower heart rate, creating optimal conditions for deep work and sustained concentration. Unlike brighter greens that may energize to the point of distraction, celadon's muted quality keeps the mind alert yet calm—the ideal state for reading, writing, and creative problem-solving.

The Ancient Ceramic Legacy

Celadon ceramics originated in China during the Eastern Han Dynasty (25–220 CE) and reached their pinnacle during the Song Dynasty (960–1279 CE). The glaze's distinctive pale green color comes from iron oxide fired in a reduction kiln. The name "celadon" itself may derive from the French literary character Céladon, who wore pale green ribbons, or from the Sanskrit word for stone. These ceramics were so valued along the Silk Road that they were traded as precious commodities alongside silk and spices.
