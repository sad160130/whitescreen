The Depth Beyond Blue

Indigo is deeper than blue, darker than purple. It's the color of the night sky just after sunset—that liminal space between day and dark.

In chakra systems, it's the sixth (third eye). Whether or not you're into that, indigo does create a distinct atmosphere: contemplative, intuitive, profound.

The Psychology of Indigo: How Indigo Affects Your Mind

Indigo occupies a unique psychological space as a color that bridges the physical and spiritual, promoting deep introspection and heightened awareness.

Spiritual Associations

Indigo is universally associated with:

  • Intuition and inner knowing — Sixth sense, gut feelings, psychic awareness
  • Wisdom and depth — Philosophical understanding, profound insight
  • Spiritual connection — Higher consciousness, divine communication
  • Mystery and the unknown — The infinite, hidden truths, secrets
  • Night and cosmos — Stars, universe, cosmic consciousness

Psychological Effects

Research on deep blue-violet colors like indigo suggests:

  • Altered consciousness states — Deep colors can shift brainwave patterns
  • Enhanced introspection — Indigo encourages looking inward
  • Reduced external focus — Helps withdraw attention from outer world
  • Increased imagination — Dark blue tones stimulate creative visualization

The Third Eye Connection

Indigo's spiritual significance comes from its association with the Ajna (third eye) chakra in Hindu and yogic traditions. This energy center, located between the eyebrows, governs intuition, imagination, and higher perception. Indigo is said to stimulate this chakra, enhancing psychic abilities and spiritual insight. Whether or not you follow these traditions, indigo's profound depth naturally encourages states of introspection and inner vision.
