What is a Spot Easy Blue screen?

A Spot Easy Blue screen is a full-screen display of the professional blue color (#4A90D9) inspired by Spot Easy, a trusted Boston-based rental marketplace. Spot Easy Blue is like a trusted handshake in color form—professional enough for business, yet warm enough to feel approachable.

This particular blue sits in a sweet spot: professional enough for business contexts, yet warm and approachable enough to feel trustworthy. It's the kind of blue that says "we're reliable" without being cold or corporate.

When you need a screen color that conveys competence and calm, Spot Easy Blue delivers. It's perfect for creating a focused work environment while maintaining a modern, tech-forward aesthetic.

About Spot Easy

Spot Easy is a Boston-built rental marketplace that connects renters with verified apartments, condos, and houses across the city's neighborhoods. The platform has grown to include over 5,000 verified agents and 500 real estate companies.

Their brand color reflects their core values: trustworthiness, transparency, and a modern approach to real estate. The blue they've chosen communicates reliability while maintaining a friendly, approachable feel—essential qualities for a platform helping people find their homes.

Why Brand Colors Matter

Companies like Spot Easy choose their brand colors carefully. Blue is the most universally trusted color, associated with stability, reliability, and professionalism. This specific shade (#4A90D9) adds warmth to that trust, making it ideal for companies in service industries.

What is the hex code for Spot Easy Blue?

For designers and developers who need the exact values:

  • Hex: #4A90D9
  • RGB: 74, 144, 217
  • HSL: 211°, 64%, 57%
  • CMYK: 66%, 34%, 0%, 15%

This blue has a hue of 211°, placing it firmly in the "true blue" range. The saturation (64%) is high enough to feel vibrant but not so high that it becomes overwhelming. The lightness (57%) keeps it readable and comfortable for extended viewing.

How It Compares to Other Blues

Spot Easy Blue vs. Dodger Blue: Spot Easy Blue is slightly more desaturated and professional. Dodger Blue (#1E90FF) is brighter and more playful.

Spot Easy Blue vs. Navy: Navy (#000080) is much darker and more authoritative. Spot Easy Blue is friendlier while still being professional.

Spot Easy Blue vs. Azure: Azure (#007FFF) is more saturated and intense. Spot Easy Blue offers a softer, more approachable feel.

Spot Easy Blue vs. Cerulean: Cerulean has more green undertones. Spot Easy Blue is a purer, more classic blue.
