What is a jade screen?

A jade screen is a full-screen display of deep green (#00A86B)—a color named after the precious stone that has been treasured across civilizations for thousands of years. Jade is the color of ancient wisdom carved in stone—simultaneously earthy and luminous, grounding and uplifting.

Unlike brighter greens that energize or darker greens that recede, jade occupies a perfect middle ground: rich enough to feel luxurious, natural enough to feel calming. It carries the quiet authority of old-growth forests and the serene balance of a still pond reflecting emerald canopy.

What does jade do to your mood?

Jade green occupies a unique place in color psychology—it combines the restorative power of green with the depth and groundedness of darker tones. The result is a color that calms without sedating, balances without neutralizing.

Emotional Associations

  • Harmony and balance — Jade promotes emotional equilibrium and inner peace
  • Renewal and growth — Associated with fresh starts and natural vitality
  • Wisdom and serenity — Evokes deep contemplation and calm clarity
  • Prosperity and abundance — Culturally linked to good fortune and wealth
  • Healing and restoration — Promotes physical and emotional recovery

Why Jade Works for Well-Being

Green is the most restful color for the human eye, requiring no adjustment from the retina. Jade's particular wavelength sits at the center of the visible spectrum, making it naturally comfortable to view for extended periods. Studies show that exposure to green environments reduces cortisol levels and promotes parasympathetic nervous system activity.

The Jade Stone Legacy

Jade has been revered for over 7,000 years across Chinese, Mesoamerican, and Maori cultures. In Chinese tradition, jade represents the five virtues: benevolence, righteousness, wisdom, courage, and purity. The stone's enduring cultural significance reflects the deep human connection to this particular shade of green.

Jade (#00A86B) has zero red, high green (168), and moderate blue (107) values. The 158° hue places it in the green-cyan range with cool undertones. Full saturation combined with 33% lightness creates its characteristic deep, precious-stone appearance that distinguishes it from brighter or more muted greens.

How is jade different from other greens?

Jade belongs to a rich family of greens, each with its own character and best use cases. Here's how jade compares to help you choose the right green for your needs:

Jade vs. Green

Jade (#00A86B): Deep, earthy, precious-stone quality with warm undertones.

Green (#00FF00): Pure primary green, highly saturated and electric. View green screen.

Jade vs. Emerald

Jade (#00A86B): More muted and grounded, with an earthy, natural feel.

Emerald (#50C878): Brighter and more vivid, with a jewel-like sparkle. View emerald screen.

Jade vs. Teal

Jade (#00A86B): Warmer green with less blue, more natural and organic.

Teal (#008080): Equal parts blue and green, cooler and more aquatic. View teal screen.

Jade vs. Forest Green

Jade (#00A86B): Brighter and more luminous, with a jewel-like depth.

Forest Green (#228B22): Darker and more muted, evoking dense woodland. View forest green screen.

Jade vs. Mint

Jade (#00A86B): Deeper and richer, more grounding and sophisticated.

Mint (#3EB489): Lighter and fresher, more playful and airy. View mint screen.
