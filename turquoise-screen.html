What is a turquoise screen?

A turquoise screen is a full-screen display of the refreshing blue-green color (#40E0D0) that brings tropical clarity to any space. Turquoise is a vacation for your eyes—tropical water clarity on demand, without the plane ticket.

That impossible blue-green you see in photos of the Maldives, the Caribbean, Bora Bora? That's turquoise. Specifically, that's light hitting calcium-carbonate sand through shallow, clear water at a specific depth. There's a reason humans pay thousands of dollars to stare at this color on vacation. Turquoise triggers a specific psychological response that researchers call "restorative attention"—the mind relaxes but stays alert. Not drowsy. Clear.

Why Turquoise Feels Healing

Turquoise's healing associations span cultures and millennia. Ancient Egyptians, Native Americans, and Persians all valued turquoise as a protective and healing stone. What is the hex code for turquoise?

For those who need precise technical details about our turquoise screen display:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #40E0D0
RGB Values R: 64, G: 224, B: 208
HSL Values H: 174°, S: 72%, L: 56%
CMYK Values C: 71%, M: 0%, Y: 7%, K: 12%
Color Name Turquoise
Wavelength ~490-520 nm (cyan-green range)

Turquoise (#40E0D0) sits between cyan and green on the color wheel, with high green and blue values and lower red. This creates the natural, water-like quality that distinguishes turquoise from pure cyan.
