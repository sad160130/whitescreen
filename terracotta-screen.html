What is a terracotta screen?

A terracotta screen is a full-display tool that fills your monitor with a warm, earthy red-brown (#E2725B)—the color of fired clay pottery that has grounded civilizations for millennia. Terracotta is the earth baked by the sun—warm, grounded, and timeless.

Named from the Italian "terra cotta" meaning "baked earth," terracotta carries the warmth of Mediterranean rooftops, ancient pottery, and sun-dried desert landscapes. Unlike the brighter intensity of coral or the deeper darkness of rust, terracotta strikes a perfect balance between vibrancy and earthiness.

What does terracotta do to your mood?

Terracotta blends the energy of coral with the stability of brown, creating a uniquely nurturing psychological effect. It's warm without being aggressive, grounding without being dull—like afternoon sunlight filtering through clay walls.

Emotional Associations

  • Warmth and nurturing — Evokes hearth, home, and handmade comfort
  • Groundedness and stability — Connects to the earth itself, promoting calm security
  • Authenticity and tradition — Associated with ancient craft and timeless artisanship
  • Creative inspiration — Stimulates imagination without overstimulation
  • Connection to nature — Bridges the gap between built and natural environments

Why Terracotta Works for Living Spaces

Interior designers have embraced terracotta for its ability to warm a space without overwhelming it. Unlike bolder reds that can feel intense or browns that can feel heavy, terracotta brings a sense of lived-in comfort and organic beauty. It pairs effortlessly with natural materials like wood, linen, and stone.

The Terracotta Revival

Terracotta has experienced a major design renaissance in recent years. From Tuscan villas to modern Scandinavian interiors, this ancient color has proven its versatility across design eras—a testament to its timeless, universal appeal.

What is the hex code for terracotta?

The hex code for terracotta is #E2725B. Here are the complete technical specifications for our terracotta screen display:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #E2725B
RGB Values R: 226, G: 114, B: 91
HSL Values H: 10°, S: 70%, L: 62%
CMYK Values C: 0%, M: 50%, Y: 60%, K: 11%
Color Name Terracotta
Color Family Red / Orange

Terracotta (#E2725B) has high red (226), moderate green (114), and low blue (91) values. The 10° hue places it in the red-orange range. Its moderate-high saturation (70%) and lightness (62%) create the characteristic warm, baked-clay appearance that distinguishes it from deeper rust or brighter coral tones.

How is terracotta different from other warm colors?

Terracotta occupies a unique space in the warm color spectrum—earthier than coral, brighter than rust, and more red than ochre. Here's how it compares to its closest relatives:

Terracotta vs. Rust

Terracotta (#E2725B): Lighter, more orange, sun-warmed quality.

Rust (#B7410E): Darker, deeper red, oxidized and weathered feel. View rust screen.

Terracotta vs. Copper

Terracotta (#E2725B): More red, matte, earthy and organic.

Terracotta vs. Coral

Terracotta (#E2725B): Earthier, more muted, grounded feel.

Terracotta vs. Ochre

Terracotta (#E2725B): More red, warmer, fired-clay character.

Terracotta vs. Brown
