What is a pine screen?

A pine screen is a full-screen display of deep dark teal-green (#01796F)—a color drawn from the heart of evergreen forests where ancient pines stand in quiet permanence. Pine is the color of walking deep into a forest where the world goes quiet—where the canopy thickens, the air cools, and everything slows to the rhythm of nature.

Named after the iconic coniferous trees that hold their color through every season, pine green carries a sense of endurance, groundedness, and natural wisdom. It sits at the crossroads of teal and forest green—darker and more complex than either, with a subtle blue undertone that gives it depth and sophistication.

Click the button above for a full-screen pine display. Whether you need a calming backdrop for deep work, a natural ambiance for meditation, or a rich green surface for photography, pine delivers a serene yet focused atmosphere.

What does pine green do to your mood?

Pine green occupies a rare psychological space—it's deeply calming like a walk through old-growth forest, yet grounding enough to keep your mind sharp and present. It doesn't relax you into drowsiness; it settles you into clarity.

Emotional Associations

  • Tranquility and calm — Pine evokes the peace of secluded forest settings
  • Stability and endurance — Associated with the evergreen's year-round constancy
  • Renewal and growth — Connects to nature's regenerative power
  • Grounded focus — Promotes concentration without overstimulation
  • Balance and harmony — Blends the calming qualities of blue and green

Why Pine Works for Focus

Research shows that exposure to green environments—even digitally—can lower cortisol levels and restore mental fatigue. Pine green's dark, muted tone is especially effective because it mimics the deep shade of forest interiors, triggering the brain's natural relaxation response while maintaining enough chromatic interest to prevent monotony.

The Evergreen Connection

Pine forests have been used for centuries in therapeutic practices across cultures. The Japanese practice of "shinrin-yoku" (forest bathing) emphasizes immersion in green environments for stress relief. A pine screen brings that principle to your digital workspace—surrounding you in the same deep green that humans have found restorative for millennia.

What is the hex code for pine?

The hex code for pine green is #01796F. Here are the complete technical specifications for our pine screen display:

Property Value
Hex Color Code #01796F
RGB Values R: 1, G: 121, B: 111
HSL Values H: 175°, S: 98%, L: 24%
CMYK Values C: 99%, M: 0%, Y: 8%, K: 53%
Color Name Pine Green
Color Family Green (Teal-Green)

Pine (#01796F) has virtually no red (1), high green (121), and moderate blue (111). The 175° hue places it in the cyan-green range, leaning slightly more toward green than blue. Near-full saturation combined with just 24% lightness creates its characteristic deep, dark, forest-like appearance.

How is pine different from other greens?

Pine belongs to the deep green family but occupies its own distinct space between teal and forest green. Here's how it compares to help you choose the right shade:

Pine vs. Teal

Pine (#01796F): Darker, more green-dominant, grounded and forest-like.

Teal (#008B8B): Brighter, more evenly balanced between blue and green. View teal screen.

Pine vs. Forest Green

Pine (#01796F): Blue-green undertone, more sophisticated and complex.

Forest Green (#228B22): Pure green, brighter, more classically "green." View forest green screen.

Pine vs. Emerald

Pine (#01796F): Much darker, more muted and earthy.

Emerald (#50C878): Brighter, more vivid, jewel-like brilliance. View emerald screen.

Pine vs. Jade

Pine (#01796F): Deeper, darker, with stronger blue undertones.

Jade (#00A86B): Lighter, warmer, more green-forward and lively. View jade screen.

Pine vs. Olive

Pine (#01796F): Cool-toned, blue-green depth, forest-inspired.
